Hi! I am Najiya Fatma. I am currently working as a Research Scientist at the [Center for Surgical and Transplant Applied Research (CSTAR)](https://med.nyu.edu/centers-programs/surgical-transplant-applied-research/) at the NYU Grossman School of Medicine, NYU Langone Health. I am currently working with Prof. [Sommer E. Gentry](https://med.nyu.edu/faculty/sommer-e-gentry) on projects that integrate simulation modeling, operations research, and machine learning to address critical challenges in organ allocation and improve patient outcomes.

I graduated with a Ph.D. in Industrial Engineering in June 2024 from the [Indian Institute of Technology, Delhi](https://home.iitd.ac.in/), where I worked with Prof. [Varun Ramamohan](https://web.iitd.ac.in/~varunr/). My Ph.D. research focused on studying patients' healthcare-seeking behavior and modeling different patient diversion mechanisms across public healthcare facility network using analytical and simulation driven machine-learning algorithms.

Please refer to my [curriculum vitae](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1X4MgKQqjBWkcFSLsJvG94OYmvZr9eKcW/view?usp=sharing) to get more details about my research and academic journey so far.
